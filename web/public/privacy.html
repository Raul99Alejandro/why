Why: privacy

The "Why decisions" Alexa skill answers questions about a small public demo log of work decisions. It does not ask for, collect or store any personal information about you.

The topic you say is sent to Amazon Bedrock only to find a matching entry in the demo log, and is not stored. The demo log is a filtered copy published by its owner: emails, phone numbers and card numbers are redacted, and names of other people are replaced by roles. Private conversations are never available to this skill.
